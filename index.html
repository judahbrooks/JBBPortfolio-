About

I was drawn to civil engineering because I love the conceptual organization behind it — taking a chaotic set of constraints and finding the underlying order that makes a structure work. What keeps me in it is the people side: at the end of the day, the roads, bridges, and buildings I help design are there to serve someone. Studying Christian Studies alongside my major keeps that purpose in view — it's shaped how I think about responsibility, service, and building things (and a life) that hold up.
